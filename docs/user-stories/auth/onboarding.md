Epic E1 · Milestone M1 · Source: [`../README.md`](../README.md)

### US-104 · Onboarding that knows why I'm here
As a **Writer**, I want a three-step onboarding that asks my intent, recommends a first program, and offers a reminder time — in under a minute — so that day 1 already has a direction. [§5.2]

- Steps: 1) Intent (understand myself / slow down / work through something / build a habit) → sets default program and day-1 tone. 2) First program, one recommendation pre-selected, alternatives behind "see other programs". 3) Reminder time picker, default 21:00 in device timezone, with a clear "not now". **[the three steps are literal]** [§5.2]
- Skippable after step 1. [§5.2]
- Step 2 plants the first rating with a scale-anchor explanation, so the gesture is learned before it matters. [§5.2]
- Acceptance: completes in < 60 s. **[screen bracket]** [§5.2]
- No account is required until first sync. **[invariant]** [§5.2]
- The reminder permission prompt lives in step 3 with a clear "not now"; never nag on later visits. [§5.2, §3.1]
