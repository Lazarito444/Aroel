### US-104 · Onboarding that knows why I'm here
As a **Writer**, I want a three-step onboarding that asks my intent, plants my first rating with the anchors explained, and offers me sync — in under a minute — so that day 1 already has a direction. [§5.2]

- Steps: 1) Intent (understand myself / slow down / work through something / build a habit) → sets the tone of the copy around the day record. 2) First rating, with the eleven anchors explained, so the gesture is learned before it matters. 3) Sync: an invitation to create an account so the journal can follow me to another device, with a clear "not now" that means it. **[the three steps are literal]** [§5.2]
- Every step carries a clear "not now" and the whole flow is skippable. [§5.2]
- Acceptance: completes in < 60 s. **[screen bracket]** [§5.2]
- No account is required until the user asks for one. **[invariant]** [§5.1, §5.2]
